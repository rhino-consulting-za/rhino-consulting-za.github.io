2. What AeroLex is

AeroLex provides searchable, offline access to South African Civil Aviation Regulations (CARs) and Civil Aviation Technical Standards (CATS), with an AI-assisted query feature and optional supplementary reference content. AeroLex is a reference and study tool only and is not for operational use; users must always verify against current official sources.

AeroLex and Rhino Consulting are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to the South African Civil Aviation Authority (SACAA) or any other government or regulatory body.

The regulations and technical standards reproduced in AeroLex are originally published by the South African government in the Government Gazette. The official, legally binding versions are made publicly available at:

  • South African Government Printing Works (Government Gazette): https://www.gpwonline.co.za/
  • SACAA Legal Notices (CARs, CATS, amendments): https://www.caa.co.za/legal-notices/

Users must consult the current official versions at the sources above before relying on any content in this app for operational, legal, or compliance purposes.

4. What we do not log

  • We do not store the text of your AI queries on our servers after a response is generated.
  • We do not log the text of items you star, bookmark, or search within our analytics events.
  • We do not collect your precise location.
  • Our analytics events do not contain information that directly identifies you by name.

5. Third parties and cross-border transfer

To operate AeroLex we use the following service providers ("Operators" under POPIA), who process information on our behalf under appropriate safeguards:

  • Google (Firebase & Google Play): app analytics, secure content delivery, app-integrity verification, and subscription billing.
  • Anthropic (AI processor): when you use the in-app AI assistant, the text of your question is transmitted to Anthropic's API to generate a response.

Cross-border transfer. Some of these providers process information on servers located outside the Republic of South Africa. In particular, AI query text is processed outside South Africa. By using the AI assistant, you consent to this transfer. We only use providers that are subject to data-protection laws or contractual safeguards that provide an adequate level of protection comparable to POPIA, as required by section 72 of POPIA.

7. Aggregated and anonymised insights

We may produce, use, and share aggregated and anonymised insights derived from app usage — for example, statistics about which areas of the regulations are most frequently consulted across the user base. We may share or sell such aggregated and anonymised insights, including in the form of reports or de-identified cohort data, to third parties such as regulatory bodies, publishers, and aviation training organisations.

This information is de-identified: it is processed so that it cannot reasonably be linked back to you as an individual, and it does not include your name, contact details, or the text of your queries. Information that has been de-identified in this way is no longer "personal information" under POPIA. By using the app you consent to the creation, use, and sharing of such aggregated and anonymised insights.

8. Retention

We keep personal information only for as long as necessary for the purposes set out above, or as required by law. Subscription and verification records are retained for as long as you hold a subscription and for a reasonable period afterward to meet legal, tax, and accounting obligations. Anonymised analytics may be retained indefinitely, as it no longer identifies you.

9. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information, including encryption of the on-device regulation database, secured communication with our backend, and app-integrity verification. No system is completely secure, but we work to protect your information against loss, unauthorised access, and misuse.
